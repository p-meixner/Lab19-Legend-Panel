# AMD — Buffett-inspired review

**Verdict: watch it.** Cash generation is attractive, but the return-on-equity
record does not meet my consistency test. Dollar amounts are USD millions.
ROE is 42.2%, 2.4%, 1.5%, 2.9%, and 6.9% across the reported years, so the
15% annual standard fails. Positive owner earnings and endpoint growth pass:
owner earnings rise from 3,220 to 6,735.

That growth is not smooth: owner earnings reach 1,121 before recovering.
Equity rises from 7,497 to 54,750 between the earliest adjacent years,
making it important to investigate the change in capital employed rather than
assume a steady economic story. The extracted table alone does not establish
the cause or whether future returns will improve. I would investigate durable
competitive advantage and capital allocation before ownership; missing total
liabilities also limits the balance-sheet assessment. No price judgment is made.

Educational persona analysis, not the actual investor's opinion.
